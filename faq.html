About the Book

Teton Angel: Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for readers

Thinking about reading Teton Angel, or picking it for your group? Here are the questions readers ask most, with no plot twists given away.

What is Teton Angel about?

When fifteen-year-old Trina Leah finds a hidden decree in her mother's cedar chest, she starts asking questions nobody who loves her is ready to answer. At the same time, a headstone on a hillside carries two words even those closest to the man buried there can't fully explain: Teton Angel. The book follows the Robinson family, their ranch, their neighbors and the secrets they keep from each other across about fifteen years.

Who wrote Teton Angel?

Roland Hunter, who was raised on a purebred livestock ranch, worked four more purebred farms and later drove commercial trucks. He began the novel around the year 2000, one paragraph at a time, and finished it while recovering from a car accident that cost him his commercial license. You can read the whole story in One Line at a Time.

Is Teton Angel Christian fiction?

Yes. It's a Christian family mystery and a western family saga. Faith runs through the lives of these characters, including prayer, church and the question of where God is when terrible things happen. Not everyone in the book is at peace with that question, and some of them are angry at God for a long time.

What is the meaning of "Teton Angel"?

It's the inscription carved at the base of a stone monument at the foot of Roy Polson's grave. The monument shows a saddle, a ball cap with the number 21 and a pair of unlaced high-top sneakers, and nobody seems to know who put it there. What the words mean is the mystery of the book, so we'll leave that one to you. You can see the symbols in the story behind the cover.

Where is Teton Angel set?

In the real country on both sides of the Tetons: St. Anthony, Driggs, Victor and Rexburg in eastern Idaho, and Jackson Hole and Rawlins in Wyoming. Our reader's map covers each place and how to visit.

Is it a historical western?

No. It's set in the modern West, with cell phones and sheriffs alongside cattle, trail rides and rodeos. If you love ranch life and western country but usually read contemporary stories, it should suit you.

Is it based on a true story?

It's a novel. The towns and the Tetons are real, and the ranch work comes from Roland's own life. For the hardest scenes, he says he relied on people who shared their emotions with him when he had no experiences of his own to draw from.

Who are the main characters?

Trina Leah and her parents, Kasey and Aiden Robinson. Conrad Robinson, the rancher at the head of the family, and his wife, Carol Lynn. Roy Polson, the rodeo kid whose name is on the headstone. And Miss Leah, who runs a hotel in Jackson Hole and knows more than she'll say. Our spoiler-light character guide covers everyone else.

How long is the book?

The print edition runs 352 pages, with 56 chapters and an epilogue. Most of the chapters are short, which makes it easy to read in small sittings.
